## Install

1. Download **Image-Lab-…-arm64.dmg** below (Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 12 or newer).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Image Lab** into **Applications**.
3. Open Image Lab. macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate.
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the Image Lab message.
   - Confirm with your password. You only need to do this once.

No Python, Homebrew or other installs needed. Everything runs offline on your Mac.

`.sha256` file: checksum for verifying the download (`shasum -a 256 -c Image-Lab-*.dmg.sha256`).
